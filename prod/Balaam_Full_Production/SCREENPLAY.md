# Balaam — screenplay and performance draft

Planned runtime 60 minutes. Scene times are initial editorial allocations. The companion shooting script and second ledger provide the complete visual directions. Dialogue is written in full.


# Episode 01 — The Lamp Above the River


## E01_SC01 — The ferry

Pethor main low landing on river-left bank looking downstream; fixed timber post at shore, low wooden ferry at waterline, bend upstream to screen left.

**E01_S001 · 6 seconds.** Ferry rests at landing. reeds drift past bow. Balaam catches loose rope.

**E01_S002 · 9 seconds.** Loose knot on post. Beor pulls strands apart. knot holds under load.

**BEOR** (patient instruction)

“Listen before you pull. The river is already telling you where it wants to go.”

**E01_S003 · 10 seconds.** Balaam steadies ferry. girl steps aboard. he offers the loose rope.

**BALAAM_Y** (gentle amusement)

“Hold this end. There. Now you have a job.”

**E01_S004 · 10 seconds.** Girl looks at water. squares her shoulders. holds rope firmly.

**GIRL_CHILD** (earnest curiosity)

“If I let go, will we float away?”

**E01_S005 · 15 seconds.** Beor watches girl climb ashore. nudges Balaam. both turn ferry around.

**BEOR** (dry humor with affection)

“She trusts you now. Bring her back with all her fingers.”


## E01_SC02 — Evening meal

Beor's mudbrick home; bench on right wall, table center-left, river-facing door behind camera-left, single clay lamp on table.

**E01_S006 · 6 seconds.** Beor tears bread. sets half beside Balaam. pushes lamp nearer.

**E01_S007 · 7 seconds.** Balaam stretches sore hand. Beor notices. Balaam conceals blister.

**BEOR** (ordinary concern)

“Tomorrow you can take the late crossing. Let that hand rest.”

**E01_S008 · 11 seconds.** Balaam glances at father's tired arm. sits straighter. reaches for empty bowl.

**BALAAM_Y** (fond challenge)

“You said that yesterday. I saw you rubbing your shoulder when you thought I was asleep.”

**E01_S009 · 11 seconds.** Beor pretends surprise. son smiles. they finish eating.

**BEOR** (wry and tired)

“Then tomorrow we shall both pretend to be strong. Put out the lamp when you're done.”

**E01_S010 · 15 seconds.** Beor settles on bench. Balaam lowers wick. room darkens around their breathing.


## E01_SC03 — The call

Same Beor house at night; bench, table and door retain their positions.

**E01_S011 · 6 seconds.** Balaam asleep. brow tightens. eyes open toward bench.

**GOD** (close calm call)

“Balaam.”

**E01_S012 · 6 seconds.** Balaam sits up. looks toward father. waits without reply.

**BALAAM_Y** (sleepy and practical)

“Father? Did you need water?”

**E01_S013 · 11 seconds.** Balaam reaches latch. second call stops hand. he opens door cautiously.

**GOD** (clear intimate authority)

“Balaam, son of Beor. Take your lamp to the old landing.”

**E01_S014 · 11 seconds.** Balaam holds wick near embers. flame catches. he shields it with left hand.

**BALAAM_Y** (quiet uncertainty)

“Who is there? I can't see you.”

**E01_S015 · 16 seconds.** Balaam scans empty lane. listens toward ridge. turns back for sandals.

**GOD** (steady deliberate English)

“A family's steering oar will break below the bend before dawn. Hang your lamp high. Give them somewhere to steer.”


## E01_SC04 — The abandoned landing

Old upper landing on river-left bank downstream of settlement bend; post above sheltered inlet, stone stairs descend to inlet. Keep current left-to-right in river-facing shots.

**E01_S016 · 6 seconds.** Balaam enters lower path. climbs three steps. pauses below old post.

**E01_S017 · 8 seconds.** Balaam lifts lamp. hooks loop. takes hand away when flame steadies.

**BALAAM_Y** (speaking into open air)

“This landing has been empty for years. Will they even see it?”

**E01_S018 · 11 seconds.** Balaam looks upstream. lips part. settles against post.

**GOD** (brief assurance)

“Stay here.”

**E01_S019 · 10 seconds.** He sits beneath lamp. draws cloak around knees. watches empty bend.

**E01_S020 · 15 seconds.** Dark water enters frame. broken oar drifts into lamplight. red strip catches on stone.


## E01_SC05 — Toward the light

Sheltered inlet directly below old post; staircase on near left, channel current beyond, family boat approaches from upstream left.

**E01_S021 · 8 seconds.** Boat emerges angled downstream. mother reaches for short oar. girl braces grain sack.

**MOTHER** (urgent controlled instruction)

“Hold the sack with your foot. Keep your hands inside the boat.”

**E01_S022 · 7 seconds.** Balaam rises. raises open hand beside lamp. calls toward boat.

**BALAAM_Y** (projected clear direction)

“Look up! Beneath the light! There's quiet water under these steps!”

**E01_S023 · 10 seconds.** Mother sights lamp. makes sustained stroke. bow begins to turn.

**MOTHER** (breath effort then relief)

“I see it. Keep still, little one. I can see where we're going.”

**E01_S024 · 10 seconds.** Line lies across lowest stair. Balaam catches it. draws slack hand over hand.

**E01_S025 · 15 seconds.** Boat rests alongside. mother lifts child onto step. Balaam steadies gunwale.

**GIRL_CHILD** (wonder breaking through fear)

“You left the light for us?”


## E01_SC06 — Someone called me

Same old ridge, post, inlet and staircase at dawn.

**E01_S026 · 6 seconds.** Balaam wrings sleeve. hears girl. looks toward lamp.

**BALAAM_Y** (simple honest answer)

“Someone called me.”

**E01_S027 · 8 seconds.** Mother holds out wet bag. Balaam declines with hand. she folds it away.

**MOTHER** (grateful and shaken)

“When you learn who it was, tell Him we made it home.”

**E01_S028 · 11 seconds.** Sunset ridge empty. Balaam enters with lamp. sits in same place.

**E01_S029 · 10 seconds.** He looks toward sky. waits. leans forward slightly.

**BALAAM_Y** (shy willingness)

“Who called me? I came back. I wanted to know if You would speak again.”

**E01_S030 · 15 seconds.** Balaam listens. shoulders relax. stays as sky darkens.

**GOD** (warm unhurried invitation)

“Come again tomorrow.”


# Episode 02 — The Jars in the Lane


## E02_SC01 — Returning

Same old ridge, post, inlet and staircase at dawn.

**E02_S008 · 10 seconds.** Neighbor opens narrow gap. notices bread. steps aside.

**E02_S010 · 15 seconds.** Balaam braces joint. neighbor taps peg. stool stands level.

**NEIGHBOR** (hesitant invitation)

## E02_SC03 — Empty jars

Pethor lane beside vulnerable mudbrick storage wall; baker's alternate stall lies around near corner, jars placed across unoccupied danger lane.

**E02_S011 · 7 seconds.** Baker lays board out. boys run past. Balaam stops by wall.

**GOD** (voice audible only to Balaam)

“Keep this lane empty until evening. Set the jars across it.”

**E02_S012 · 6 seconds.** He carries one empty jar. turns it upside down. sets it across lane.

**E02_S013 · 11 seconds.** Hadan approaches barrier. stops with basket. looks over jars.

**HADAN** (public dry humor)

“Are we selling the road now? What does a man pay to walk through?”

**E02_S014 · 11 seconds.** Balaam remains by jar. meets merchant's eyes. opens palm toward alternate lane.

**BALAAM_Y** (plain request)

“Walk around today. I was told to leave this stretch clear until evening.”

**E02_S015 · 15 seconds.** Baker lifts board. boys follow her around corner. Balaam moves remaining basket.

**BAKER** (brisk everyday direction)

“You two can argue over the crumbs where I can see you.”


## E02_SC04 — Waiting for meaning

Pethor lane beside vulnerable mudbrick storage wall; baker's alternate stall lies around near corner, jars placed across unoccupied danger lane.

**E02_S016 · 6 seconds.** Dust sifts from hairline crack. lands on empty ledge. faint grit falls.

**E02_S017 · 7 seconds.** Hadan rests weight on sack. Balaam watches lane. merchant gestures at jars.

**HADAN** (amusement becoming curiosity)

“You could at least tell me what we are waiting for.”

**E02_S018 · 11 seconds.** He starts to answer. stops. looks back toward wall.

**BALAAM_Y** (honest without embarrassment)

“I don't know yet.”

**E02_S019 · 11 seconds.** One mudbrick falls. upper stack gives way. dust spreads into empty lane.

**E02_S020 · 15 seconds.** Baker pulls boys against her. checks their faces. holds them near.

**BOY1** (frightened misunderstanding)

“We didn't break it. We were over here.”


## E02_SC05 — The offer of thanks

Pethor lane beside vulnerable mudbrick storage wall; baker's alternate stall lies around near corner, jars placed across unoccupied danger lane.

**E02_S021 · 6 seconds.** Balaam lifts intact jar. brushes dust from rim. sets it beside ruined board.

**E02_S022 · 9 seconds.** Hadan opens pouch. tips silver into palm. holds it out.

**HADAN** (sincere gratitude)

“Take it. We would have been standing there. Someone ought to give you something.”

**E02_S023 · 10 seconds.** Balaam looks from silver to baker. folds merchant's hand gently shut. points to broken stall.

**BALAAM_Y** (matter-of-fact kindness)

“She has lost her board. Buy her timber. She still has to work tomorrow.”

**E02_S024 · 10 seconds.** Baker looks toward Hadan. Hadan nods offscreen. she exhales into boy's hair.

**E02_S025 · 15 seconds.** Hadan takes one end of beam. Balaam lifts other. they carry it out together.

**HADAN** (apology through humor)

“Next time, tell me which jars to carry. I made you do all the lifting.”


## E02_SC06 — The hill without a question

Old upper landing on river-left bank downstream of settlement bend; post above sheltered inlet, stone stairs descend to inlet. Keep current left-to-right in river-facing shots.

**E02_S026 · 6 seconds.** Balaam sits. rubs bruised heel. looks into darkness.

**BALAAM_Y** (relieved intimate recollection)

“The little one thought he had broken the wall.”

**E02_S027 · 6 seconds.** He glances toward settlement. smiles. lets smile fade into rest.

**E02_S028 · 11 seconds.** He leans head back against post. eyes close briefly. listens.

**GOD** (gentle familiar direction)

“Go home. Your father is saving supper.”

**E02_S029 · 11 seconds.** Beor lifts bowl. Balaam takes it. both look toward repaired stall beyond lane.

**BEOR** (protective tenderness)

“People have started asking when you will speak. You are still allowed to eat first.”

**E02_S030 · 16 seconds.** Balaam sits beside father. door stays open toward river. lamp lights both faces.

**BALAAM_Y** (quiet contentment)

“I didn't go up to ask anything tonight. I just wanted to be there.”


# Episode 03 — The City Six Days Away

Same low landing in winter; clothing is arranged on dry high stones above spray.

**GOD** (simple direction)

“Bring dry clothes. Lay them above the wet line.”

**WOMAN** (uncertainty with concern)

“This was for my brother. Who are we waiting for?”

**E03_S003 · 11 seconds.** Balaam looks upstream. returns gaze to woman. holds out dry cloth.

**BALAAM_Y** (unforced honesty)

“I haven't been given a name. Help me keep these out of the spray.”

**E03_S004 · 11 seconds.** Raft clears bend. soaked survivors crouch together. woman recognizes one man.

**WOMAN** (recognition breaking into cry)

“That's him. That's my brother!”

**E03_S005 · 15 seconds.** Woman wraps tunic around brother. holds his face. he trembles under cloth.

**BROTHER** (cold breath between words)

“The ferry broke loose. We thought the bend would carry us past everyone.”


## E03_SC02 — A room glimpsed

Old upper landing on river-left bank downstream of settlement bend; post above sheltered inlet, stone stairs descend to inlet. Keep current left-to-right in river-facing shots.

**E03_S006 · 6 seconds.** Balaam looks at lamp. hears distant voice. raises head.

**TRAPPED_WOMAN** (weak but intelligible remote voice)

“Tell my son I'm still here.”

**E03_S007 · 6 seconds.** Blue bowl on floor. dust falls beside split beam. mat shifts beneath debris.

**E03_S008 · 11 seconds.** Balaam approaches collapsed house. son blocks opening. Balaam studies bowl beyond.

**SON** (certainty masking fear)

“She went to her sister. We looked here already.”

**E03_S009 · 11 seconds.** He sees bowl. kneels at doorway. turns toward son.

**BALAAM_Y** (steady invitation)

“She said, tell my son I'm still here. Call to her once more.”

**E03_S010 · 16 seconds.** Son kneels. calls beneath beam. hand appears at gap.

**SON** (voice breaking with relief)

“Mother? It's me. Stay where you are. We can hear you.”


## E03_SC03 — Keep the lamp

Same house at evening; open door provides a line toward river and lane.

**E03_S011 · 8 seconds.** Beor sets bread down. son reaches for lamp. father tilts head toward supper.

**BEOR** (affectionate complaint)

“Take it with you if you mean to make it cold again.”

**E03_S012 · 6 seconds.** Balaam smiles. lifts lamp. turns toward lane.

**BALAAM_Y** (playful promise)

“I'll be back before you finish that piece.”

**E03_S013 · 11 seconds.** Balaam loosens straps. lifts feet clear. sets sandals together.

**GOD** (calm specific request)

“Take off your sandals. Keep your lamp.”

**E03_S014 · 10 seconds.** He steadies flame. head turns toward river. listens closely.

**GOD** (intimate urgency)

“A child is under a stair. His mother is looking for him.”

**E03_S015 · 15 seconds.** Feet rest on ridge stone. hard match cut changes ground to ash. feet shift at unfamiliar sound.


## E03_SC04 — The burning street

Fictional distant city, workshop alley leading toward gate; exterior stair beside workshop and child hidden beneath.

**E03_S016 · 7 seconds.** Balaam stands with lamp. crowd passes toward gate. woman searches against their flow.

**CITY_MOTHER** (projected distress)

“Has anyone seen my son? He went back into the yard!”

**E03_S017 · 7 seconds.** Balaam catches her attention without grabbing. points toward workshops. she looks.

**BALAAM_Y** (clear fast questions)

“Where is the stair? Show me the stair outside the workshop.”

**E03_S018 · 11 seconds.** Lamp enters low gap. child becomes visible. puppy moves inside tunic.

**E03_S019 · 10 seconds.** Boy sees mother. pulls puppy free. offers it ahead of himself.

**BOY1** (ash-dry frightened voice)

“I couldn't leave him. I thought you would be angry.”

**E03_S020 · 15 seconds.** Mother gathers child to side. follows Balaam's lamp. emerges into square.

**CITY_MOTHER** (firm breathless protection)

“Hold my sleeve. Both of you. Keep coming.”


## E03_SC05 — Six days

Open square safely beyond workshop fire, water jars near exit, path back to alley visible.

**E03_S021 · 6 seconds.** Mother notices bare feet. offers cloth. Balaam looks around square.

**BALAAM_Y** (quiet disorientation)

“What city is this? I don't know these streets.”

**E03_S022 · 9 seconds.** Mother names destination on a passing caravan's tag offscreen. looks back at him. studies lamp.

**CITY_MOTHER** (puzzled calculation)

“Where did you come from? There are six days of road between here and Pethor.”

**E03_S023 · 10 seconds.** Balaam looks toward smoky sky. tightens lamp handle. shakes ash from foot.

**BALAAM_Y** (still trying to understand)

“Someone heard you calling.”

**E03_S024 · 10 seconds.** Balaam steps away from square. match cut returns ridge. sandals remain aligned below him.

**BALAAM_Y** (quiet prayer across transition)

“Where do You want me to go now?”

**E03_S025 · 15 seconds.** Balaam enters. father stops chewing at ash on feet. son sits slowly.

**BEOR** (increasing astonishment)

“You have been gone less than a meal. Where did that ash come from?”


## E03_SC06 — The story travels

Same Pethor market after wall repair, with caravan unloading area beyond.

**E03_S026 · 8 seconds.** Trader dismounts pack donkey. asks direction. spots Balaam near doorway.

**TRADER** (curious recognition)

“A woman sent me looking for a man with a river lamp.”

**E03_S027 · 9 seconds.** Trader unwraps small cloth gift. sets it on table. gestures toward road.

**TRADER** (warmly incredulous)

“She asked everyone. Someone heard your name here. Her boy wanted you to have this.”

**E03_S028 · 9 seconds.** Balaam unwraps roughly modeled puppy. thumb follows uneven ear. hand stills.

**E03_S029 · 10 seconds.** Balaam sets clay puppy beside lamp. smiles into open darkness. sits down.

**BALAAM_Y** (tender amusement)

“He made it too small to carry anyone through a fire. But I think he tried.”

**E03_S030 · 14 seconds.** Balaam stays by post. distant caravan leaves lower road. light remains small above water.


# Episode 04 — The Prophet at the Door

Same house in daylight; the queue waits outside the river-facing doorway.

**E04_S002 · 8 seconds.** Neighbors wait with wraps and baskets. grown girl offers water. Balaam greets nearest visitor.

**GIRL_ADULT** (friendly practicality)

**NEIGHBOR** (contained panic)

**BALAAM_A** (decisive companionship)

**E04_S005 · 15 seconds.** Searchers follow gully. boy appears on ledge. father stops before running forward.


## E04_SC02 — The answer withheld

Same house in daylight; the queue waits outside the river-facing doorway.

**E04_S006 · 7 seconds.** Trader sets route markers down. Balaam sits with empty hands. waits.

**TRADER** (frustration under politeness)

“I have waited three days. Tell me which road to take.”

**E04_S007 · 10 seconds.** He looks at markers. listens. raises eyes to visitor.

**BALAAM_A** (patient honesty)

“I have no word for you today. I can share what I know of the roads.”

**E04_S008 · 10 seconds.** Trader reaches for pouch. stops. studies Balaam.

**TRADER** (embarrassed suspicion)

“People said you always had an answer. Have I brought too little?”

**E04_S009 · 9 seconds.** Beor sets bowl at each man's elbow. trader withdraws pouch. Balaam accepts spoon.

**BEOR** (unshowy protection)

**E04_S010 · 14 seconds.** Three men begin eating. queue shifts outside. Balaam asks trader a quiet question.

**BALAAM_A** (human interest)

## E04_SC03 — Basket on the ledge

**E04_S011 · 8 seconds.** Balaam sets basket on high dry stone. tests stability. steps back.

**E04_S012 · 10 seconds.** Balaam points toward basket. neighbors look at clear sky. mother lifts tool.

**E04_S015 · 14 seconds.** Balaam wipes sweat. sees debris before water rises. turns to call neighbors.

**BALAAM_A** (clear urgency)

## E04_SC04 — Water reaches the basket

**E04_S020 · 15 seconds.** Families share dry space. Balaam gives cloak away. sits beside father.

**MOTHER** (grateful exhaustion)

## E04_SC05 — His father at home

Same house at evening; open door provides a line toward river and lane.

## E04_SC06 — First night of waiting
