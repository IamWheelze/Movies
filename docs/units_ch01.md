# Chapter 1, "The lamp above the river": units (one continuous 8 to 10 s take each)

Rules: each unit starts from the last frame of the one before it (same place, same people, camera only moves).
A new first image is made only where the SCENE changes. N = narrator (one fixed calm voice). "..." = spoken on screen.

## Scene 1: Ferry mornings (Pethor river, dawn)
| Unit | What the camera sees (one take) | Narrator / line |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Wide dawn over the Pethor river. A small ferry crosses slowly, an old man at the oar, a boy sitting near the bow. | N: Before kings sent men to ask for his blessing, Balaam spent his mornings on a ferry with his father. |
| 02 | The camera drifts closer as the ferry glides to the landing stones. The hull knocks against stone. The old man tilts his head. | N: Beor could tell how heavily a boat was loaded by the sound it made against the landing stones. |
| 03 | Beor, close, still at the landing. He listens, then speaks. | Beor: "Hear that? She is heavy on the left." |
| 04 | Balaam, close, beside him. His eyes close as he listens to the water. | N: He taught Balaam to listen for an oar knocking against a hull in the dark. |
| 05 | Reeds drift on the current and slowly turn as it changes. Balaam's eyes follow them. | N: And to watch where the floating reeds went when the current changed. |
| 06 | Balaam, close, opens his eyes and answers. | Balaam: "I think I know what it is." |
| 07 | Beor, close, smiles gently. | Beor: "Listen a little longer." |
| 08 | Both on the landing in the dawn light. Balaam closes his eyes again. | N: Balaam was good at listening. Whenever he thought he already knew what he was hearing, Beor made him listen a little longer. |
