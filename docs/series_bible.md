## Consistency rules
1. One approved keyframe per shot, made from the cast tags above, then animated by LTX from that frame.
2. The voice sentence is copied word for word into every speaking prompt. Each line is transcribed after generation; a line that does not match is re-rolled.
3. Every shot is 5 to 8 seconds, longer when the line is long: line length + 1.5 s before and after.
4. Locations are described with the same words in every shot of a place (see each episode file).
5. No shot has more than two speakers; two-person scenes are cut as alternating singles.
6. One caption style, one music bed, one loudness for the whole series.
